# Governance

EvidenceRadar-Editions has three authoritative layers:

1. **Acquisition and identity code** controls direct source queries, local scope filtering and deduplication.
2. **Canonical edition JSON** controls all displayed article content and translation state.
3. **Validated archive** controls public Pages publication and history.

A supplied EvidenceRadar checkout may provide source hints and a reference commit. It does not provide the publication corpus.

Bibliographic presence is not a verified scientific claim. Translation basis and source access depth must remain explicit. Changes to scope semantics, identity, translation binding, archive revision policy or publication validation require tests and documentation.
